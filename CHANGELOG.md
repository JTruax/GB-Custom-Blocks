# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-16)

- New: **Video Banner**. A GenerateBlocks Container with a video file as its background (Media Library or URL) and an optional poster image.
- New: "Disable on mobile" and "Disable on tablet". The video is never downloaded on those screens.
- New: Pause toggle part (`aria-pressed`), keyboard operable, styled in GenerateBlocks.
- New: Reduced motion and Save-Data mean no autoplay. Lazy loading near the viewport; pausing off-screen and in background tabs.
- New: Default GB Pro Global Styles: `boim-video-banner`, `boim-video-banner--overlay`, `boim-video-banner__content`, `boim-video-banner__toggle`.
